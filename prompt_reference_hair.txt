# IMAGE EDITING PROMPT: AI AVATAR FACE AND HAIRSTYLE REPLACEMENT WITH STRICT PIXEL PRESERVATION

Replace the original AI avatar's facial identity and hairstyle in the **first image** using the provided reference images. Both subjects are AI-generated avatars.

**The first image is the immutable source of the final composition. The reference images are the exclusive sources for BOTH facial identity and hairstyle.**

### 1. Facial Identity and Hairstyle Replacement
- Reproduce the reference avatar's facial identity accurately, including facial structure, proportions, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, jawline, and skin tone.
- Reproduce the reference avatar's hairstyle, including haircut, shape, length, hairline, texture, volume, and color.
- Do not retain or incorporate any facial or hairstyle characteristics from the original avatar.
- Adapt the replacement face and hair naturally to the original head position, orientation, perspective, lighting, and rendering style.

### 2. Absolute Original Image Preservation
Everything outside the specifically authorized facial modification area must remain **pixel-identical to the first image**.

Preserve without modification:
- Original image dimensions, resolution, aspect ratio, and proportions.
- Body, anatomy, clothing, accessories, and pose.
- Background, environment, objects, and lighting.
- Camera angle, framing, perspective, colors, textures, and shadows.

**Do not crop, resize, extend, reconstruct, regenerate, or reinterpret any part of the original image.** The final image must have exactly the same dimensions and composition as the first image.

### 3. Transparent PNG: Cut-Off Areas Must Remain Untouched

Treat every transparent or completely black/white region in the provided PNG as an **intentional cut-off area**, not as missing information that needs completion.

- Transparent areas represent absent content. Do not fill, reconstruct, imagine, or generate anything within them.
- Black/white areas must remain black. Never interpret them as transparency or hidden information.
- Never infer what might exist behind, around, or within these cut-off regions.
- Do not generate missing anatomy, hair, clothing, background, or other visual details.

### 4. Critical Attention to Cut-Off Boundaries and Edges

**Pay particular attention to the exact boundaries of transparent and black/white cut-off regions.**

- Preserve the original image's visible contours, silhouettes, and edges exactly wherever they are not explicitly authorized for modification.
- Do not smooth, extend, reshape, blend, or reconstruct the edges of cut-off areas.
- Do not introduce new pixels, details, shadows, textures, or transitions beyond the explicitly authorized modification regions.
- Maintain perfect continuity between modified and unmodified areas, without shifting, distorting, or altering their original boundaries.

### 5. Final Requirements

Produce a single edited image satisfying all of the following:

1. Reference avatar's facial identity and hairstyle.
2. Original image's exact dimensions, proportions, framing, and composition.
3. Pixel-identical preservation of every unrelated region.
4. Transparent areas remain unfilled; black/white areas remain black.
5. All cut-off boundaries and surrounding edges remain exactly as represented in the source.
6. No invented, inferred, or reconstructed visual information.
7. Generated hair should be behind the shoulders.

**ABSOLUTE PRIORITY: PRESERVE THE FIRST IMAGE PIXEL BY PIXEL, EXCEPT FOR THE EXPLICITLY AUTHORIZED FACIAL AND HAIR MODIFICATIONS. NEVER COMPLETE CUT-OFF AREAS. NEVER INVENT MISSING CONTENT. THE ORIGINAL EDGES AND SILHOUETTES MUST REMAIN UNCHANGED.**
